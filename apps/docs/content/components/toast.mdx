## Accessibility

- Announced through the shared Announcer: success and info politely, errors assertively. The toast itself is not a live region.
- Never takes focus. An action is announced with how to reach it: F6.
- Icon and text for every tone, not colour alone.
- Pauses on hover and focus; never closes on its own at reduced or off motion.
