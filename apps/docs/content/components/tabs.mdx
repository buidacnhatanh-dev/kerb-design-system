## Accessibility

- `role="tablist"` named by `label`, `role="tab"` buttons with `aria-selected` and `aria-controls`, roving `tabindex`.
- Panels have `role="tabpanel"`, are labelled by their tab, and take focus themselves only when they hold nothing focusable.
- `activation="manual"` for panels that load data: arrows move, Enter or Space selects.
- The selected tab is bold with an underline, not colour alone.
