## Accessibility

- A native `<button>`, `type="button"` unless you pass `type="submit"`.
- Disabled uses `aria-disabled="true"`, not `disabled`, so the button stays in the Tab order and people can find out why. The click is blocked.
- `disabledReason` shows in a tooltip on hover and focus and is linked with `aria-describedby`.
- Loading keeps the label as the name, sets `aria-busy` and announces “Loading” through the Announcer.
- At least 44 by 44 pixels to press, also at the small size and compact density.
