## Accessibility

- A `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>`: the legend is the group’s name.
- Native radios with one `name`, so the browser gives the arrow keys and the single Tab stop.
- Each option’s hint is its description, outside the label.
- The focus ring shows on the radio a keyboard user reaches, also after an arrow key in Safari.
