## Accessibility

- A native `<dialog>` opened with `showModal()`, named by its title and described by its description.
- On open, focus goes to the safest action, here “Keep project”. Destructive dialogs never focus the destructive button first.
- The rest of the page gets `inert`, so screen readers and Tab can’t leave the dialog.
- On close, focus returns to the trigger, or to a fallback if the trigger was removed.
- `placement="bottom"` makes it a sheet for small screens, as the Preferences sheet does.
