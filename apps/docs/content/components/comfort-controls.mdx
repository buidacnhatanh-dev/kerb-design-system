## Accessibility

- Native `<input type="range">` with a visible label; the value shows next to it and is the `aria-valuetext` (“18 pixels”).
- Changes are announced politely after the range rests for half a second.
- The controls keep their size; only the preview and the rest of the app follow the settings.
- Background sets the theme: White is light, Warm is comfort, Dark is dark.
