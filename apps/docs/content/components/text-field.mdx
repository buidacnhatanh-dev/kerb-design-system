## Accessibility

- The `<label>` is always visible. A placeholder is never the label.
- Hint and error are linked with `aria-describedby`, in that order. The hint is never inside the label.
- An error sets `aria-invalid="true"` and shows a red border, an icon and text. It is not a live region: the form announces a summary on submit.
- The show password button keeps its name and uses `aria-pressed` for its state.
- 44 pixels tall to press at every density.
